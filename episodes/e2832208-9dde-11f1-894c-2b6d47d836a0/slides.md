# Stripe 收购 OpenRouter：NFT 到推理的硬转向

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-3 mt-4 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-blue-50 border-l-4 border-blue-500">
<strong>事实卡片</strong><br/>
OpenRouter 由 OpenSea 创始人之一创立；公司成立"little over two years old"；本轮被 Stripe 收购。主持人评价这是"crypto pivots to AI"中最成功的案例之一。
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-green-50 border-l-4 border-green-500">
<strong>他们做对了什么</strong><br/>
"nailed the timing, nailed the product"——在推理需求爆发的时间点，做出了人人爱用的产品。
</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-4 p-4 rounded bg-yellow-50 border-l-4 border-yellow-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">

"They built a marketplace with providers of inference and consumers of inference over the API. And just totally crushed it."

<div class="text-xs opacity-60 mt-1">
— Erik Voorhees 评价 OpenRouter：在 API 之上搭建推理供需两端市场
</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-3 text-sm">
OpenRouter 是"full pivot out of crypto"——完全离开加密世界；Venice 则把 crypto 融进 AI 产品。两条路线，这是本期的起点。
</div>

# 两条路：硬转向 vs 融合

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 mt-4">

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-red-400 bg-red-50 bg-opacity-30 text-sm leading-relaxed">

### 硬转向派
- OpenRouter：NFT → 推理 API（主持人："full pivot out of crypto. Like goodbye."）
- Hermes：几年前也彻底退出 crypto
- 逻辑：AI 需求太猛，crypto 是负资产

</div>

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-green-500 bg-green-50 bg-opacity-30 text-sm leading-relaxed">

### 融合派（Venice）
- 把 token 融进 AI 产品，而不是离开 crypto
- 主持人：Venice 是 crypto + AI 项目里"the leading example we have right now"
- 逻辑：crypto 的 primitives 应该进入大众产品

</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-4 text-sm leading-relaxed">
主持人向 Voorhees 提问：为什么发 token 而不是像 OpenRouter 一样完全转向？他的回答从一句"我无法转出 crypto"开始……
</div>

# "我无法转出 crypto"

<div class="p-4 rounded bg-blue-50 border-l-4 border-blue-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">

"I can't pivot out of crypto... it flows through my blood."

<div class="text-xs opacity-60 mt-1">
— Erik Voorhees，回答"为什么 Venice 还要发 token"
</div>

</div>

<div class="text-sm leading-relaxed mt-4">

- crypto 是"a new way for money to move around the world"，而钱是所有商业活动的基础
- 他不希望 crypto 自成一个生态——**最好的 primitives 应该"离开 crypto 世界、进入普通产品"**
- 目标是展示：这些技术不只是投机工具，而是**激励机制**

</div>

<div class="mt-4 p-4 rounded bg-green-50 border-l-4 border-green-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">

"if it's all just like crypto for crypto people serving crypto products with crypto, then we've kind of missed it"

<div class="text-xs opacity-60 mt-1">
— 只服务加密人的加密产品 = 错过真正目标；"There's a lot of ways to do them badly, but there are some ways to do them well"
</div>

</div>

---

# Token 的真正价值：给用户"股份"

<div class="text-sm leading-relaxed mt-4">

用 crypto 支付只是"table stakes"——就像 Visa 或 Stripe 一样的支付方式，没什么好兴奋的。**更有意思的是让用户在一个平台里拥有 stake。**

</div>

<div class="p-4 mt-4 rounded bg-yellow-50 border-l-4 border-yellow-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">

"All the major AI labs are private. You know, with the exception of Google."

<div class="text-xs opacity-60 mt-1">
— 除 Google 外所有 AI 实验室都是私有的，普通人无法分享 AI 创造的财富
</div>

</div>

<div class="text-sm leading-relaxed mt-4">

- 人们看着 AI 创造巨额财富，却"wondering when their jobs are going to be stolen"
- 这完全可以避免：**用户应该是参与者，而不是消费者**

</div>

<div class="mt-4 p-4 rounded bg-purple-50 border-l-4 border-purple-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">

"if your users can be participants in it with you instead of just consumers of the thing"

<div class="text-xs opacity-60 mt-1">
— 用户参与平台成长，是更公平也更有趣的公司构建方式
</div>

</div>

# Token vs Equity：两个资产，卖哪个？

<div class="space-y-3 mt-4 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-blue-50 border-l-4 border-blue-500">
<strong>第一阶段</strong><br/>
"we started Venice just as a normal company"——Wyoming 公司，一年自筹资金，没融过资
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-green-50 border-l-4 border-green-500">
<strong>第二阶段</strong><br/>
一年后发布 VVV，"never sold it"——token 一枚没卖
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-yellow-50 border-l-4 border-yellow-500">
<strong>第三阶段</strong><br/>

</div>

<div class="mt-4 p-4 rounded bg-purple-50 border-l-4 border-purple-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">

"We decided to sell the equity. We'll sell that, we'll keep the tokens."

<div class="text-xs opacity-60 mt-1">

</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-3 text-sm leading-relaxed">

# 激励一致性：公司自己就是最大 holder

<div class="text-sm leading-relaxed mt-4">

很多人以为 equity 持有者和 token 持有者是"两个群体"——但 **公司每个人都同时持有两者，而公司自己持有的 token 比任何人都多**。

</div>

<div class="mt-3 p-4 rounded bg-blue-50 border-l-4 border-blue-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">

"Every dollar that we spend burning tokens, is that harming our equity holders? No. I'm the biggest equity holder of all, why would I do that?"

<div class="text-xs opacity-60 mt-1">
— 燃烧 token 是在帮股权，因为"the company itself has more tokens than anyone"
</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-3 p-4 rounded bg-yellow-50 border-l-4 border-yellow-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">

"Bro, I am the token holder."

<div class="text-xs opacity-60 mt-1">
— 但在 Twitter 上没人信："they can't get the incentive alignment there"；"Most of our customers are not crypto people"
</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-3 text-sm leading-relaxed">
答案不是吵架，而是透明：token 流向、供给、燃烧量全部上链——"we're just empirically demonstrating it with time"。
</div>

<div class="p-4 mt-4 rounded bg-red-50 border-l-4 border-red-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="text-xs opacity-60 mt-1">

</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-3 p-4 rounded bg-green-50 border-l-4 border-green-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="text-xs opacity-60 mt-1">

# Meme coin 的监管悲剧

<div class="text-sm leading-relaxed mt-4">

监管把激励彻底扭曲了：**有真实 utility 的 token 反而更容易被认定为证券**，于是正经项目不敢发 token，meme coin 大行其道。

</div>

<div class="mt-3 p-4 rounded bg-red-50 border-l-4 border-red-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">

"If you had a project that had utility to it and you had a token, it had a higher chance of being deemed a security than if you just released a meme coin."

<div class="text-xs opacity-60 mt-1">
— 监管倒置：越正经越危险
</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-3 p-4 rounded bg-yellow-50 border-l-4 border-yellow-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">

"It's not accretive to society. It is a form of entertainment that is consumptive."

<div class="text-xs opacity-60 mt-1">
— meme coin 投机不增加社会价值；DeFi summer 之后，真实项目的 token 再没成为主流
</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-3 text-sm leading-relaxed">
主持人 Kain（Synthetix 创始人）自述：2019 年把 Synthetix 做成纯 DAO、关掉所有实体，"our lawyers are like, you are insane"。而 Voorhees 的总结是："the incentives are structured so that you're better off buying a meme coin"。
</div>
