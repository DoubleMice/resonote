# 财政部对伊朗宣战："经济 D-Day"

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 mt-4 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-red-400 bg-red-50 bg-opacity-30">

### 公报内容
- 对与伊朗做生意的国家和实体推动次级制裁，霍尔木兹海峡持续封锁
- 传统上财政部长谈的是"未来两年会发生什么"，现在按分钟影响市场（Kane 转述 Matt Levine 的玩笑）

</div>

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-yellow-400 bg-yellow-50 bg-opacity-30">

### 伊朗的加密底色
- 伊朗因外汇管制拥有庞大的比特币经济，大型中心化交易所几经波折
- 公报向购买伊朗黄金、石油或比特币的人发出警告："anyone who's like buying Iranian gold or oil or Bitcoin is going to have some problems"

</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-4 p-4 rounded bg-blue-50 border-l-4 border-blue-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<strong>Peter</strong>："One doesn't normally announce D-Day before they have a D-Day."—— 但公报的措辞注定"significant and uncompromising"

</div>

<div class="mt-3 text-sm opacity-80 leading-relaxed">
<strong>作者概括</strong>：Peter 承认美国整体上过度使用制裁，但也认为经济手段好过全面开战——"sanctions is probably a great alternative to just going all out in war"

# 公报没提 DeFi，也没说 DeFi 没事

<div class="space-y-3 mt-4 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-blue-50 border-l-4 border-blue-500">
公报原文没有点名任何项目，但把 <strong>digital assets and digital asset intermediaries</strong> 列入了次级制裁考察对象的清单——这是 DeFi 第一次进入官方视野
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-yellow-50 border-l-4 border-yellow-500">
<strong>Kane 的观察</strong>：X 上有人指出，"they left the door open for even DeFi"——Uniswap 能不能和伊朗人交互？Ethereum 能不能？没人回答
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-red-50 border-l-4 border-red-500">
<strong>Kane 的底线</strong>："They didn't say that DeFi wasn't in trouble, which you know probably should be somewhat concerning."——没排除，本身就是信号
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-green-50 border-l-4 border-green-500">
<strong>Peter 转述公报的宽泛措辞</strong>：effectively any source could be a target——"Any source of the regime's illicit revenue and every economic lifeline"
</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-3 text-sm opacity-80 leading-relaxed"><strong>作者概括</strong>：公报的留白既是法律技术，也是政治信号——既没给 DeFi 免罪金牌（"doesn't give anyone a clear pass"），也没承诺不碰</div>

<div class="text-sm leading-relaxed space-y-3">

# 未排除的极端：节点、矿工与开发者

<div class="space-y-3 mt-4 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-red-50 border-l-4 border-red-500">
<strong>两难处境</strong>：如果伊朗用比特币网络和 DeFi 工具变现被套牢的石油储备，算不算"经济生命线"？行政分支会打到哪一层？
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-orange-50 border-l-4 border-orange-500">
<strong>三个层次</strong>：先打有机构身份的中心化实体，还是干脆瞄准非托管基础设施——节点、中继（relayers）、矿工，最极端的是"写软件的开发者"
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-blue-50 border-l-4 border-blue-500">
<strong>Peter</strong>："They haven't ruled that out. But again, who would put forward a press release saying we're not going to do the crazy thing?"

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-yellow-50 border-l-4 border-yellow-500">

# 务实主义：终结"以执法代替监管"

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 mt-4 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-blue-400 bg-blue-50 bg-opacity-30">

### 风向已变
- DOJ 去年发布备忘录：停止对符合 FinCEN 指引的"非托管"个体提起无照资金传输起诉——"if you're not custodial you're not a money transmitter"
- 同一时期，执法火力转向 cartel money brokers 等真人目标

</div>

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-yellow-400 bg-yellow-50 bg-opacity-30">

### 效率逻辑
- Kane：关停 Ethereum 不是结果导向的做法——"even if you had no idea what you were doing... that's probably not going to be bang for your buck"
- Peter：行政分支在追求效率最大化——"these are the real people that we can exercise real jurisdiction over"

</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-3 p-3 rounded bg-green-50 border-l-4 border-green-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">
<strong>Peter</strong>：按帕累托法则，抓人类经纪人能覆盖 80% 的通道，附带损害最小；抓协议则又贵又伤无辜——这与"regulation by prosecution"的终结一脉相承
</div>

# 稳定币：绕行制裁的天然向量

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 mt-4 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-orange-400 bg-orange-50 bg-opacity-30">

### 为什么是稳定币
- 既中心化、又能在去中心化网络上自由流动——绕制裁的理想载体
- 监管者必然找 Circle、Tether 谈话："what can you do here?"

</div>

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-blue-400 bg-blue-50 bg-opacity-30">

### 公链可审计
- "public blockchains that are auditable where we can watch in real time as money flows to known sanctioned addresses"
- 不需要非公开情报，chain analysis 就够了；Uniswap 前端多年来一直在做制裁合规

</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-4 space-y-2 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-green-50 border-l-4 border-green-500">
<strong>Peter</strong>："the less gray that Tether has gotten... the more useful an instrument Tether is for... the dollarization of the world"——灰色度越少，美元化工具越有用
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-yellow-50 border-l-4 border-yellow-500">
<strong>结论</strong>：把 Tether 逼到躲着美国政府，比让它来 DC 对话更糟——"If you force Tether into... avoiding the US government instead of going to DC, like that just seems worse"
</div>

</div>

# GENIUS 时代的「二级市场」难题

# Roman Storm：重审推迟到 2027 年 4 月

<div class="space-y-3 mt-4 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-blue-50 border-l-4 border-blue-500">
<strong>最新进展</strong>：Roman Storm 在制裁与洗钱指控上的重审，从 10 月改期到 <strong>2027 年 4 月</strong>——因为法官正在考虑他的 Rule 29 无罪动议（要求整案撤销）；"It's sort of like a hurry up and wait situation"
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-yellow-50 border-l-4 border-yellow-500">
<strong>一审结果</strong>：无照资金传输罪——陪审团判有罪；制裁与洗钱两项——陪审团无法达成一致，宣布 mistrial。"Doesn't mean he's innocent, doesn't mean he's guilty."
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-green-50 border-l-4 border-green-500">
<strong>核心法律问题</strong>：什么是 money transmitter？简单答案——"Can you control customer funds? Yes, you're a money transmitter. If you can't, no, you're not."；SDNY 的答案要复杂得多
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-red-50 border-l-4 border-red-500">
<strong>为什么重要</strong>：判决将决定开发者能否预判自己的软件是否违法——"there's really hard legal issues at play here"
</div>

</div>

# "平底锅不存热，但导热"

<div class="mt-4 grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-red-400 bg-red-50 bg-opacity-30">

### SDNY 的推理
- "a frying pan doesn't hold the heat but it transmits the heat"——用"传导"来定义资金传输
- Peter 的困惑："How do I know if... based on that kind of legal reasoning"——这种标准让开发者无法预判

</div>

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-green-400 bg-green-50 bg-opacity-30">

### 两套标准之争
- <strong>简单标准</strong>：控制客户资金 = money transmitter；不控制 = 不是
- <strong>复杂标准</strong>：传导热量、便利交易也算——一审陪审团在此标准下仍难以达成一致

</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-4 p-3 rounded bg-yellow-50 border-l-4 border-yellow-500 text-sm leading-relaxed">
<strong>作者概括</strong>：陪审团对后两项罪名悬而未决，本身说明这套"导热"理论连陪审员都难说服——但 SDNY 选择重审，而不是放手
</div>

# Berman 修正案：制裁止步于"交易与信息"

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 mt-4 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-green-400 bg-green-50 bg-opacity-30">

### 法定的言论豁免
- 美国制裁法有明确例外：总统制裁权 "doesn't have the authority to sanction transactions and information"
- 源自众议员 Berman 的修正案——制裁不该成为限制言论自由的钝器

</div>

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-blue-400 bg-blue-50 bg-opacity-30">

### 先例
- 迈阿密的古巴画商：卖画违反古巴禁运，但法院反复敲打行政分支对"信息豁免"的狭窄解释
- 转播足球赛赚钱不构成制裁；在美国出版伊朗官员的小说也不构成

</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-4 space-y-2 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-yellow-50 border-l-4 border-yellow-500">
<strong>应用</strong>：Roman 交易的不是资金，而是软件与链上信息——"publishing software and information on the blockchain... that's all information"
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-red-50 border-l-4 border-red-500">
<strong>金句</strong>："You can't regulate the guy who wrote software that other people used to move Ethereum because that is information, even if the Ethereum movements are transactions."
</div>

<div class="p-3 rounded bg-blue-50 border-l-4 border-blue-500">
<strong>Peter 的限定</strong>：这不是"比特币交易只是信息"的天真论证——"I don't mean it in the naive way"
</div>

</div>

# "为什么就是不肯放手？"

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 mt-4 text-sm leading-relaxed">

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-red-400 bg-red-50 bg-opacity-30">

### SDNY 的自我定位
- 有 "the global financial cop on the block" 的名声——跨境金融犯罪的首选拦截点，这是他们的骄傲（"that's the chip on their shoulder"）
- 诱因很硬：Tornado Cash 流过大量朝鲜黑客资金，"money that might end up financing nuclear weapons"——"think that's my bread and butter, I'm gonna go get that"

</div>
